Club FAQ

A few things to know before your next game.

How do I join the roster?

Ask the club admin to add your name. You start at 1000 Elo and do not need an account.

How long is a match?

Play to 11 points and win by at least two. Give the admin the final point score so close games count fairly.

How are results recorded?

Tell the admin the winner, opponent, and final score. Ratings and records update when the result is saved.

How does the score affect Elo?

A win over a stronger opponent moves your rating more than a win over a weaker opponent. Close games move ratings less than decisive wins.

New players move faster: K is 30 for the first 10 matches, 20 for matches 10–29, and 16 from match 30 onward. Each player uses their own K, so the winner’s gain and loser’s loss can differ. Both are capped at 30 points, and a rating cannot drop below 100. An expected win can produce no rating change.

For two new players at equal ratings, 12–10 changes each rating by 9 and 12–0 by 15. A 1400-rated player with 30 matches beating a new 1000-rated player 12–0 gains 1 point; the opponent loses 3.

E = 1 / (1 + 10^((opponent Elo − winner Elo) / 400))
M = 0.5 + 0.5 × (winner points − loser points) / winner points
Each change = min(30, round(player K × (1 − E) × M))

New results use this club adaptation of Elo. Earlier ratings and match history stay as recorded.

Can a result be corrected?

Ask the admin. Newly entered matches can be undone in reverse order, restoring both players’ previous ratings and records. Matches involving a deleted player cannot be undone.
