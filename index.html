Club Format

  1. Every registered player begins at 1000 ELO.
  2. Report the result immediately after the match.
  3. The ELO change depends on the ratings entering the match.
  4. Higher-rated players are expected to win; an upset creates a larger rating swing.
  5. Both players must confirm the reported result before the match becomes official and changes ELO.
  6. Rankings update automatically after both confirmations are recorded.

ELO Formula

Expected score: 1 / (1 + 10 (opponent ELO − player ELO) / 400 )

New ELO: old ELO + K × (actual score − expected score)

This prototype uses K = 32.
