Club FAQ

A few things to know before your next game.

How do I join the roster?

Ask the club admin to add your name. You start at 1000 Elo and do not need an account.

How long is a match?

Play to 11 points and win by at least two. Give the admin the final point score so close games count fairly.

How are results recorded?

Tell the admin the winner, opponent, and final score. Ratings and records update when the result is saved.

How does the score affect Elo?

Beating a stronger opponent earns more points. Close games produce smaller rating changes than decisive wins: at equal ratings, 12–10 changes Elo by 9, while 12–0 changes it by 16.

We use K = 32 with a score multiplier:
E = 1 / (1 + 10^((opponent Elo − winner Elo) / 400))
M = 0.5 + 0.5 × (winner points − loser points) / winner points
Change = max(1, round(32 × (1 − E) × M))

The winner gains the change and the loser loses it, with a minimum rating of 100. This applies to new results; earlier ratings stay as recorded.

Can a result be corrected?

Ask the admin. Newly entered matches can be undone in reverse order, restoring both players’ previous ratings and records.
