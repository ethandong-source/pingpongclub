How the Club Rating Works

Every new player begins at a baseline of 1000 ELO. After the admin records a competitive match, the winner gains rating points and the loser loses points, with a minimum rating of 100.

Our rating system uses the standard Elo algorithm (K = 32): beating a higher-rated player yields a larger rating surge, while winning against a lower-rated player rewards fewer points.

Admin-recorded results: The admin enters the winner, loser, and score. Ratings update immediately when the result is saved.

Club Rules & Guidelines

  1. Baseline: Every registered club player starts at 1000 ELO.
  2. Match Length: Standard matches are played to 11 points (must win by at least 2 points).
  3. Match reporting: Tell the admin the winner, loser, and score after the match.
  4. Ratings: Elo and win/loss records update immediately when the admin saves the result.
  5. Corrections: Ask the admin to correct a result. New matches can be undone in reverse entry order.
  6. Minimum rating: A player’s Elo cannot drop below 100.

Elo Rating Formula

Ratings are calculated using the standard Elo rating formula with a K-Factor of 32:

1. Expected Win Probability:
EWinner = 1 / (1 + 10 (Loser Elo − Winner Elo) / 400)

2. Rating Change:
Δ Elo = Round( 32 × (1 − EWinner) )
Interactive Test: If a 1000 Elo player defeats a 1100 Elo player, expected score is ~36%, resulting in a +20 Elo gain!
