How the club works

Players start at 1000 ELO. After every official match, the winner gains rating points and the loser loses the same amount.

Beating a higher-rated player is worth more.

Club Format

  1. Every registered player begins at 1000 ELO.
  2. Official matches are best-of-3 games unless the club announces otherwise.
  3. Report the result immediately after the match.
  4. The ELO change depends on the ratings entering the match.
  5. Higher-rated players are expected to win; an upset creates a larger rating swing.
  6. Rankings update automatically after an official result is recorded.

ELO Formula

Expected score: 1 / (1 + 10 (opponent ELO − player ELO) / 400 )

New ELO: old ELO + K × (actual score − expected score)

This prototype uses K = 32.
